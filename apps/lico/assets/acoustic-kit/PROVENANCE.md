# Acoustic drum kit samples

These one-shots come from Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer
Samples, performed by Austin McMahon. The source recordings and these edits
are available under CC0 1.0 Universal. The middle tom is a separate rack tom take pitched down four semitones.
Each source was converted to 32 kHz mono and trimmed from 2 ms before its
first 4%-of-peak crossing. The edited samples use short raised-cosine fades
and a peak level of 75% full scale before 16-bit PCM encoding.
