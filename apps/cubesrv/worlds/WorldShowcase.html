FIELD NOTES / GENERATION & BUILD

Make a little world.
Give it room to grow.

Build cameraDrag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Right-drag to pan. Home frames the world. Alt-drag also orbits while placing or removing. World layoutEach world remains 4 × 4 × 4 chunks. Each chunk is 64 × 64 × 64 c4 cells, or 512³ c1 units. The full world is now 2048³ c1 units: eight times the previous side length. Cube tier sizes themselves are unchanged. Place / removePlace a c1 control cube at the center of a new platform. Placement addresses snap to the c4 terrain grid; the control is centered on the actual footprint. Its platform, growth, and local branch belong to it. With tower demos visible, click the tower to select or remove the same owner. Keys 1 / 2 / 3 choose Select / Place / Remove. Deleting an owner does not remove other platforms or the main network. Platform sizePlatforms and paths both use c4 cubes (8 c1 per side) on the same lattice. The six generated portal plateaus independently roll 16 / 17 / 18 / 19 c4 across. Each side plateau is seeded horizontal or vertical, using only the two planes perpendicular to its portal face. Top and bottom use XY or YZ. The attached 2 × 1 connector shares the same roll. Placed platforms keep their pinned rolls when the world regenerates. Cube-size towersSeven cubes, one per tier: bottom to top r3 (12), c4 (8), r2 (6), c3 (4), r1 (3), c2 (2), c1 (1). Each tower is 36 c1 tall and centered on its platform. Inspect tower zooms in and labels each cube. These display-only rulers do not affect routes, collision, or budgets; toggle them off to reveal manual control cubes. Small-cube growthOptional growth is off by default. Each platform retains its independent 250–500% roll. When enabled, it adds connected c3 (4 c1) shelves, exactly half the side length of the c4 terrain cubes. No extra cube tiers are introduced. Local branchesWith automatic paths on, each owned platform gets one link to existing generated terrain. In a blank world, the first cube grows an isolated platform; later platforms connect to earlier ones. With automatic paths off, the platforms remain and only the paths are hidden. Removing an empty-world anchor repairs only dependent links. World 27 offers all six colours plus Void. Undo / redoCtrl or Cmd + Z / Shift + Z. History is independent for every world. Ctrl or Cmd + Y also redoes. Portal labThe Portals tab owns eight reusable presets: Sky, Underground, black-hole, white-hole, Island, City, Special, and Leave. Portal structure defaults by destination: pure-theme and Leave portals use Terraced, two-theme portals use Twin ring, and three-theme plus Special/Void portals use Tri ring. Any preset can override that structure; opening, depth, rotation, and flat-colour settings remain reusable. Portal inspectionChoose a face, then Back or Angle to inspect that actual portal using the build camera. Back looks through the frame from its reverse side. The World tab or Home frames the whole scene. Amber and mint guide cubes identify the authored player spawn and forward direction; the optional passage guide previews the full route. Guides add no terrain and do not count toward the budget. Portal pathsPortal frames remain round. Each outer face supplies one portal in its X, Y, or Z plane, with one plateau 15–25% of the way toward the world centre. Side plateaus seed between their horizontal and vertical perpendicular planes; top and bottom use XY or YZ. Generation stamps exactly six straight portal-to-plateau links—no cross-links and no separate portal bridge. Each is one continuous 2 × 1 c4 path from the outermost in-bounds world-grid slice to its platform edge, sharing the platform roll. Rendering, picking, and collision use those same c4 cells. Portal frames stay c2 and flush with the world boundary. Save / importExports store all 27 world recipes, eight portal presets, corrected cube tiers, the 512-c1 chunk size, c4 placement-grid addresses, and c1-world-space branch centerlines. Earlier projects are migrated by enlarging their world-space layout eightfold; v15 c2 portal designs are preserved without a second size increase. The old r3 (6) and r4 (12) labels become r2 (6) and r3 (12). Safe placed-platform recipes are kept; invalid local links are rebuilt. Imports are fully validated before the live project changes.

All 27 worlds start completely empty. Generation is explicit. Control cubes and their owned platforms survive regeneration. Remove a control cube to remove its platform, growth, and branch; the generated main network is separate. 16 is a soft budget, not a hard cap. JSON includes generation recipes, eight reusable portal presets, cube colours, pinned owned-platform rolls, and saved local branch centerlines—not a game-specific loader.
