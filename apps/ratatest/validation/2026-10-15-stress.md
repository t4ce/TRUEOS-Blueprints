## Fix and limits

The earlier raw probes demonstrated that native CSI `@`, `P`, and `X` were
ignored. They now insert/delete/erase within the current row, preserve moved
styles and cursor positions, clamp counts, and use the existing background
erase policy. Seven new parser regressions include every stream split of a
three-row editing specimen; the app also replays its printed raw examples.

Live counters measure completed application/backend draws and emitted bytes.
The selected 40 fps is a target; display/presentation rate was not measured.
SSH captures use separately recorded synthetic origin CPR replies and bypass
the native ANSI cell projection. Native correctness here is backed by actual
parser/bridge tests and real-byte replay, rather than a claim of complete
pixel-level inspection. Unicode width, unsupported native text modifiers and
several terminal extensions remain documented specimens.
