Preserved experiment archive

probes-part-1.zip and probes-part-2.zip together contain every entry
from the original probes.zip, with the original paths and contents.

To restore the complete archive, extract both ZIPs into the same directory:

    unzip probes-part-1.zip -d restored-probes
    unzip probes-part-2.zip -d restored-probes

Each ZIP can also be opened individually. Some experiment directories span
both archives, so extract both for the complete collection.
